# Data-source research

Checked November 4, 2026. These are source assessments, not imported production data.

## Hosting

[GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) confirms static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript hosting. A browser application with local persistence fits this deployment. Scheduled data collection would run outside the visitor's browser, such as a repository workflow, and publish validated static data.

## Official rules and conflicts to resolve

- [Official Solo Ops announcement](https://www.arenabreakoutinfinite.com/en/news.html?content_id=69683e0ca7baca4e7caa42ea4d544a329a0e) describes playing as a team of one. Squad generation must exclude incompatible solo-only modes.
- [Official Season 7 announcement](https://www.arenabreakoutinfinite.com/en/news.html?content_id=d25657b0ad464a4634a91bea70f57c69e66f) was identified through the [readable announcement mirror](https://patchbot.io/games/arena-breakout-infinite/articles/1423-season-7-update-announcement). Direct official page extraction returned only a shell.
- The mirrored announcement states a 900,000 Lockdown ceiling, level-5 protection/penetration caps for Lockdown, level-4 caps for Normal, temporary replacement of selected map/difficulty combinations by infected modes, and mastery changes. These are candidate versioned rules, not a complete current availability matrix. Confirm later patches and actual lobby rules before implementation.
- [Official January announcement](https://www.arenabreakoutinfinite.com/en/news.html?content_id=68cc0dcea20b6a4c80a929faea1e18245698) is indexed with entry-value floors different from some newer community guides. Never combine old floors with newer ceilings without tracing effective versions.
- Owner-supplied entry-value behavior distinguishes equipped from stored items, including designated loaded magazines packed for entry. Preserve this product requirement while verifying game reference value independently from vendor and marketplace purchase prices.
- Basic free secure-container dimensions, magazine footprints, per-ammo stack caps, storage restrictions, and all mode/map availability remain verification tasks.

## Recommended data direction, pending implementation discussion

Maintain a project-owned, versioned static catalog with explicit provenance and verification status. Use source adapters for candidate updates rather than relying on a third-party website at roll time. Keep approved manual corrections separate from imported facts. Publish small validated changes automatically and prepare reviewable updates for new items or gameplay rules. Current price coverage and full attachment dependencies remain the main feasibility gaps; do not promise live prices or complete coverage yet.

## Follow-up: direct source and browser inspection

The following findings supersede the earlier access limitations where noted. No production dataset was imported and no maintainer was contacted.

- ABI Tracker's weapon and market pages returned successfully through a direct public-page request, and the rendered weapon, armor, and market views were inspected in a normal browser.
- The tracker links to a [public asset repository](https://github.com/hexaov91/abi-assets). The [weapon-data commit history](https://github.com/hexaov91/abi-assets/commits/main/data/weapon-detail.json) shows an October 28, 2026 update. The displayed game-data version remains 1.0.0.151.17, dated October 27. Neither date alone proves every item matches the live game.
- Its weapon table rendered 95 base rows and 139 attachment comparison rows. Base rows include duplicate display names, a shield, and a signal gun, so 95 must not be described as 95 distinct eligible firearms. Recent guns such as A545 and AK-15SC were visible.
- The [public weapon page script](https://github.com/hexaov91/abi-assets/blob/main/_abi/releases/js/GameInfo/weapon.js) uses compatibleAccessoryIds when present and falls back to tag matching. Its displayed attachment rows are filtered to selected stat effects. Those rows are not a complete attachment catalog or a full graph of mounts, required parts, slot conflicts, and dependencies.
- The armor table rendered 177 rows spanning protection-related items, with material, protection level, protected areas, and movement/hearing modifiers. Count is not a verified count of unique available armor pieces.
- The rendered [market page](https://abi-tracker.azurewebsites.net/Market/View?minorId=20210) showed 5.45x39mm BS at 7,134 Koen, a price ladder, quantities, and a reported update timestamp of November 4, 2026, 22:40 UTC-8. Record this as the site's reported timestamp, not independently verified collection time. One other ammo entry showed no price, so coverage is incomplete.
- That ammo detail also showed weight, a 1x1 footprint, damage, penetration, armor damage, velocity, projectiles per shot, and a stable numeric item identifier. Its contact sale price of 1,288 is explicitly a factory-condition table value excluding server sell-rate overrides, wear, and attached parts. It is not a verified vendor purchase offer.
- The public repository includes weapon, bullet, armor, item-index/detail, throwable, workbench, localization, and seasonal-mission files. Files ending in .json are protected binary payloads, not plain reusable JSON. The site's public loader handles them for normal display. No protected payload was decrypted or republished during research, and no supported external export/API agreement has been established.
- Only dependency licenses were found in the inspected repository tree, with no project-wide reuse license identified. Obtain a supported export and clear reuse terms from the maintainer before selecting this as a production feed.
- All five entries on the [announcement index](https://abi-tracker.azurewebsites.net/Announcement/Index?lang=en) were read in the browser. They discuss launcher troubleshooting, workbench sharing, corrected item data, throwable calculations, and equipment build-code behavior. No public API integration instructions or data-reuse permission were found.
- The [October 21 correction](https://abi-tracker.azurewebsites.net/Announcement/3?lang=en) says T191/T192 incorrectly referenced U191-only barrels, and M4A1 incorrectly referenced an M16-only barrel. This is concrete evidence that compatibility requires cross-checking even on an actively maintained source.
- The [October 2 build-code announcement](https://abi-tracker.azurewebsites.net/Announcement/4?lang=en) says codes are not always available and worn armor is omitted because the game buys new armor when applying a code. Do not promise in-game build-code export from arbitrary site loadouts.
- The [November 3 workbench announcement](https://abi-tracker.azurewebsites.net/Announcement/5?lang=en) explains copying player IDs to view shared research and a daily refresh of workbench results. This is not permission to reuse item data and does not establish the market refresh schedule.
- Further inspection of [WilliamFerrant's weapon data](https://github.com/WilliamFerrant/abi-api/blob/master/data/api/weapons/weapons.json) found 74 weapon records. Its [weapon_mods file](https://github.com/WilliamFerrant/abi-api/blob/master/data/api/weapons/weapon_mods.json) contains 208 rows including repeated names, with names/category/image references rather than the required compatibility rules. It cannot independently support full attachment generation.
- The separate ABIDB CSV repository's latest inspected commit is August 12, 2026. The [ABIDB web repository](https://github.com/fabiopsyduck/ABIDB-WEB) has September 20 activity and bundled equipment CSVs, but no attachment dataset in its inspected CSV directory. Repository activity must be distinguished from per-file game-data freshness.

The preferred cache design is a bundled last-verified snapshot plus browser caching, with a daily repository update check and reviewed semantic diffs. Upstream outages must never replace valid data with empty files. This design depends on having authorized, stable source access; browser-visible records alone do not establish that access.

## Owner-confirmed permission and initial delivery

On November 4, 2026, the owner confirmed MIT licensing for most sources and permission from the others for the initial local cache. On clarification, the owner confirmed this covers all discussed sources and public inclusion of data and icons in the GitHub Pages site and repository. The owner described icons as edited screenshots from the game. This is owner-provided authorization; it is not an independent license audit.

Proceed with initial approved snapshots and preserve applicable license notices. Include a Credits tab linking every data/asset source and crediting the game for screenshot-derived icons, in addition to item-level references. Automated upstream collection and live updates are deferred until the owner obtains separate permission after building and sharing the project. The previously proposed daily update workflow is a later capability, not enabled initial behavior.

## Visual and sound prototype references

- [B3 Universal storage layout](https://abibuilder.com/wiki-assets/gear/chest_rig_layouts/B3_Universal_Chest_Rig_Layout.webp) was directly inspected. It depicts eight distinct 1x2 pouches in a two-by-four arrangement, with visible interior cell lines. The prototype now follows those proportions and dividers; this does not verify the layouts of other rigs.
- [Kenney Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) identifies its license as Creative Commons CC0. The included [license copy](https://github.com/GeorgeQLe/assets-sfx-combat/blob/main/LICENSES/kenney-impact-sounds-license.txt) was inspected before using sampled metal, wood, and soft impacts from the publicly mirrored pack. Prototype files retain the license and credit Kenney. Source is Kenney; the mirror is a distribution source, not the original creator.
- Original military-case state artwork and an armory insignia were created for the design preview. Their use does not establish a final approved identity or production animation asset pipeline.
